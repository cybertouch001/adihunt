AdiHunt

Walk the real world. Shadows spawn on a live map around you — get close, fight, extract them into your Legion, raid dungeon gates and claim territory.

How to play

  1. Sign up, allow location, and open the map — shadows are all around you (dark violet glows; brighter = rarer).
  2. Walk within 40 m of a shadow and tap it to engage. A short turn-based fight: strike with good timing, guard against heavy hits, then bind it when it is weak.
  3. Extract shadows into your Legion, level them up with essence, evolve them, craft relics.
  4. Do daily quests, keep your streak, climb the global and weekly leaderboards.

Game modes

Day / Night cycle
Local time changes the map: night shadows are stronger and rarer, day shadows weaker.
Dungeon Gates
Portals open for a few hours. Clear 4 timed waves and a boss — solo, or call friends with the raid horn 📯.
Hunter Guilds
Found or join a guild, chip away at a weekly guild boss together, climb the guild board.
Territory Claim
Beat a zone guardian to claim it in your colour. Zones decay daily and reset every week.
Trading & gifting
Add friends by code and swap shadows when you stand near each other.
Global events
Eclipse Hour, Blood Moon, Double XP and more — switched on by the admin.
Relic crafting
Collect shards, craft relics, equip a blade, cloak and sigil.
Hunt of the Day
One bounty shadow each day for double rewards.
QR beacons
Shops and events display a QR; scan it for a daily bonus.
Ghost trail
Replay your last 24 hours of walking as a glowing ghost on the map.

Play safe

Never walk while looking at the screen. Stay off roads and rail lines, stay out of water, respect private property, and only play where you are allowed to be. You are responsible for your own safety. No demo or in-game reward is worth an accident. A daily distance reminder nudges you to take breaks.

No GPS? Demo mode

Inside the app tap the 🚶 button to simulate walking (arrow pad or tap-to-walk). Rewards are reduced in demo mode, and the server still checks speed so teleporting is rejected.

Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors. Shadows only appear where the game places them — you need to be physically in an area to hunt there.
